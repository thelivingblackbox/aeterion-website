What drives us

Life leaves a trace.

Living systems carry a record of their own history. Most molecular measurement is single-timepoint and destructive: prior states are inferred rather than read, and the information that would explain how a system arrived at its present state is largely discarded at the moment of measurement. Aeterion's research is directed at that record, at how it might be accessed, read and interpreted, and at what a deeper, more dynamic understanding of biology could mean for research and for real-world application.
